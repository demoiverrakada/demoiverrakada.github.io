About

I like experiments that make it difficult to fool yourself.

I’m Umang, a software engineer and independent AI-safety researcher based in India.

I work on behavioral auditing, model evaluations, finetuning forensics, and mechanistic interpretability. The recurring question across my research is whether an attractive measurement actually tracks the behavior or construct we care about.

That has led me to causal activation interventions, blinded model audits, sandbagging detectors, matched-pair benchmarks, and fine-tuning pipelines with frozen holdouts. I try to build the instrumentation myself, preregister consequential decisions, and treat a failed control as a reason to stop—not as an invitation to rewrite the claim.

Alongside independent research, I work in software engineering at Amazon. I studied Electrical Engineering at IIT Delhi, where I also worked on formally verified electronic voting.

Current interests

  • Behavioral auditing of finetuned models
  • Evaluation awareness and sandbagging
  • Activation-based monitoring and control
  • Reliable model-based evaluation
  • Benchmark design and measurement validity

Tools I use
