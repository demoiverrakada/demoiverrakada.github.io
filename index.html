How I work

Negative results are results.

A clean stop can be more informative than a polished but unstable claim. My projects use frozen protocols, explicit gates, matched controls, and post-hoc labels that stay visibly post-hoc.

  1. 01

    Freeze the question

    Define the estimand, intervention, success threshold, and failure interpretation before opening the result.

  2. 02

    Audit the instrument

    Verify decoder parity, model behavior gates, data boundaries, and controls before trusting a headline metric.

  3. 03

    Report the boundary

    Separate what the experiment supports from what it merely suggests—and preserve failed branches as evidence.
