Black-box vs. white-box sandbagging detection

Exploratory pilot · Aug 2026 · Sandbagging, access-aware detection

Compared three detectors on Qwen2.5-3B: behavioral inconsistency, mechanism-motivated weight noise, and a labeled activation probe. The black-box detector separated prompted sandbagging in aggregate, but detected nothing at an empirical 1% false-positive threshold in a 100-question sample. Near-perfect probes likely learned prompt identity; a corrected train/validation/test rerun remains open.

  • WMDP-Bio accuracy, neutral → blatant / target: 66% → 27.5% / 40.5% (200 items)
  • Target-strategy black-box AUROC: 0.79
  • Empirical true-positive rate at 1% false-positive rate: 0

Limits: these are exploratory point estimates, not evidence of a deployment-ready low-FPR detector.

Evaluation-awareness control validation

Stopped before training · Sep 2026 · Evaluation awareness, experimental validity

A hash-locked semantic validation over 520 blinded contexts stopped before training when supposedly neutral controls retained evaluation-conditioned meaning. Near and distant placebos were contaminated at 15% and 5%, invalidating the proposed causal estimand.

Lesson: lexically neutral reasoning inside an awareness-bearing trace may still be causally downstream of evaluation awareness.

Refusal Cartography

Stage 0 · Jul 2026 · Refusal behavior, black-box and white-box

Probes a model's refusal boundary with controlled, mostly benign prompts, aiming to fit an explicit model of what drives refusal, and to test whether that model predicts attacks it has never tried better than a matched search baseline. It is at Stage 0: building and checking the grading instrument, which separates whether a model refused from whether its answer carried real capability.

LLM red-teaming evaluation pipeline

May 2026 · Refusal robustness

A local pipeline measuring how often refusals hold in Llama 3.2 3B, with Llama 3.2 1B as an automated judge. On 500 adversarial prompts the attack success rate was 48.4%, and 23% of 100 benign prompts were wrongly refused.

Limits: a small local judge labels the outputs, so the rates carry judge noise.

AI alignment and safety benchmarks

Development slice complete · Sep 2026 · Safety evaluation, threat modeling

A broader evaluation suite organized around three distinct questions: what a model can do, what it will do under declared conditions, and whether control measures can stop it. The first development run, covering 20 families × 6 variants = 120 prompts, is complete. The hidden validation tooling is implemented, one private family has passed the current review workflow, and the remaining bank has not yet been authored.

Limits: the mixed-provenance development labels are not independent human gold; they support pipeline debugging, not model-safety or ranking claims.

Refusal is mediated by a single direction (Arditi et al. 2024)

Aug 2026 · Mechanistic interpretability

On Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, removing one "refusal direction" from the activations cut refusals on harmful prompts from 83% to 8%, and adding it made the model refuse 96% of harmless prompts, up from 4%.

Limits: 24 evaluation prompts per condition.

Toy models of superposition, with sparse autoencoders

Aug 2026 · Mechanistic interpretability

Reproduced Elhage et al. (2022), then trained sparse autoencoders to recover the hidden features and graded them against the known ground truth, before training an SAE on TinyStories-1M. It illustrates a known pitfall: an SAE can reconstruct well while finding the wrong features.

Induction heads in GPT-2 small

Jul 2026 · Mechanistic interpretability

Found and causally checked the induction circuit, following the ARENA material: the top induction head is 5.5 and the previous-token head feeding it is 4.11. Ablating the heads raised loss on repeated tokens, and ablating 4.11 lowered 5.5's induction score from 0.92 to 0.69.

Local LLM fine-tuning lab

Engineering study · 2026 · ML engineering, frozen evaluation

Built an MLX/QLoRA pipeline for a structured, evidence-grounded Python documentation assistant. A V3 adapter remains selected after four later adapters were rejected for cross-holdout utility regressions, despite improvements in validation loss or decision accuracy.

  • Versioned datasets, untouched holdouts, and checkpoint gates
  • Strict schema validity improved from 33.3% to 100% on the 90-case V3 evaluation; the base already achieved 100% on the core semantic metrics
  • Explicit rejection of V4–V7 regressions
